# Glider · original body with saturated blue wings

Art: original Glider pixels from `roster-source.png`, extracted in the original puppet at `88ffc23`. New wing material generated by OpenAI `image_gen.imagegen` (model ID not exposed), registered back onto existing wing canvases with JavaScript. The battlefield concept from `0f5539c` informed its broad electric-blue wingspan.
All 11 non-wing parts and their glows are restored byte-for-byte to the original roster-derived puppet. Original dark body, warm bone textures and blue seams replace the light cyan shell treatment. Wings now use saturated royal/electric blue, original folds and blue veins; broad colour remains visible with glow disabled.
Manifest unchanged byte-for-byte, all 26 base/glow canvases unchanged. Existing filled-wing masks and small edge chips are retained. Same part names, offsets, pivots, parents, z-order, rig and animations. Skitter art unchanged; no game code changed.
168 scripted joint contacts pass at ±20°/±6° and ±25°/±8°, including crossed poses. **No joint gaps observed or flagged**; no clipping or green fringe. Exact original-body/canvas checks: `blue-validation.json`.
Browser: `/dev/td-aids.html` as-is and `/dev/td-roster.html?view=grid`, Chromium device scale 2, zero page/load errors. At 45px the broad blue membranes read on tan floor and dark rock, including glow off, with no tint/rim/underglow aids. `blue-game-size-2x.png` is the Glider-only crop of the full game-size screenshot.
Shared captures refreshed in `../skitter-parts/preview-aids-2x.png` and `../skitter-parts/preview-roster-2x.png`. `comparison.png` shows original grey-wing roster against this blue-wing puppet. Prior crystal atlases/tools and their reports are historical experiments, superseded by this pass.
