# Scrapjet Rally

Design discussion #293 and playable PR #294. The original cloudcup ID retains arcade history and best finishes.

Race the breaker crew on Magnet Mile or Kiln Switchbacks. On the grid, paddles select Rookie, Hotshot or Outlaw, B cycles the course, ↑ cycles 3, 12 or 24 laps and A starts. The crew's nominal pace corresponds to 55%, 78% and 102% FTP. The chassis banks into lateral motion with damped roll. Between 30 Hz simulation ticks the chase camera, bikes and rider poses use the same display fraction. The road eases its bank across survey joints. A short paddle squeeze trims the line; a hold leans the bike. Speed builds with a lag and carries through pedal release. Cornering load pushes the bike outward, the shoulder costs speed and boost strips reward a clean line.

Follow a rival's exhaust to draft. The point rider contests the tow while two flankers seek striking distance. Their pace adjustment has a bounded range, so they can be dropped. A flanker holds its line and winds up before sweeping. A sweeps left, B sweeps right. Both directions work without a second steering input. A miss puts the chain into recovery. A counter staggers the attacker for 2.4 seconds. A hit overloads the player's jet and requests a temporary 2.5% SIM grade. After landing a hit, the attacker takes point on the contested line and the flankers move ahead. From the hit, the crew waits the seven-second overload duration plus 20/16/13 seconds by class before attacking again. Two seconds at 110% FTP clears the overload early; it also clears naturally after seven seconds.

The road mesh, bank forces, camera, scenery and actors sample the selected closed, arc-length-resampled course. The rider leans into a turn, braces one hand on the bar and swings from the other shoulder. The chain follows the swinging glove. WebGL2 via Three.js draws the race; audio uses Wattcade's shared effects and continuous-voice buses. The panorama, suit paint, track material and original salvage paintings were created with ImageGen. The trackside turbines, car heaps and signal towers use shared geometry and a weathered enamel atlas. Each has a fixed orientation on the course. Run `python scripts/scrapjet-enamel.py` to rebuild the atlas from the three original yard paintings.

Hardware checks still pending: iPad/Bluefy frame pacing, paddle trim, warning readability, trainer overload and sprint recovery.

The PR preview includes /dev/scrapjet.html for an immediate test ride. Add ?course=kiln to open on Kiln Switchbacks. On the grid, X changes course, ↑ changes distance and Z starts. In the race, left/right steer, Z/X sweep and Space simulates a sprint. The footer supports touch. To ride with hardware, use the normal arcade entry.

Each Magnet Mile lap forks at the broken viaduct. Mint-painted shoulders trace the left service bridge through the bend and back to the main deck. It works at any pace. The right ramp crosses 24 course units in one second of flight, so the HUD calls for 87 km/h at the lip. Amber folded-steel rails end at the lip. The chevron plates point across a working slag pour to a mint-striped landing. The speed gauge marks launch speed on approach. Touchdown compresses the hover suspension and pays a boost. The later single span narrows to less than half the normal width. Deck geometry, support tests, flight and recovery use the same cross sections for all four bikes. Switch attempts the ramp on Hotshot and Outlaw. A fall costs 2.15 seconds of forward progress. The yard crane catches the falling bike, lifts it over the rail and drops it on supported deck at the same race distance. Pedaling gives the rider a rolling restart, with 2.3 seconds of contact protection.

Kiln Switchbacks runs through two firing drums at night and descends into an exposed cooling flume. Its solid deck, survey stakes, pressure vessels and pickups are specific to the works. All four bikes can charge the copper seam. Cover 64% of a firing run within its edges while pedaling at 80% of the selected class pace to release a 3.8-second boost at the exit. Switch contests the seam. A center line stays open at every effort. A seed-3 Hotshot at 78% FTP finished three laps in 2m20s tracing the seam and 2m27s riding the center. The night panorama, steel plate and firebrick materials use original ImageGen art. The rotating collars, steam, signs, lighting and firing sounds run in the race renderer.

The incoming-chain rattle pans toward the attacker. A side warning grows toward contact, leaving the route and recovery cues readable in the center.

Rivals adjust their class pace by up to 20% in either direction outside a 14-unit gap. Salvage boxes can grant armour or overdrive. When trailing the leader by more than 22 units, a box has a 75% chance of granting six seconds of extra speed from pedaling. Hold C in the test ride to cut simulated power and cadence.
