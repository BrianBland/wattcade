# Dynamo Defense (towerdefense)
CONTRACT-REQUESTs: none.

## Seams
- Levels (docs/towerdefense/LEVELS-DESIGN.md): `sim/level.ts` holds a map as data (`TdLevel`: waypoints, pads, turret, hive, flyer spawns, style, optional painting) and derives `TdGeometry` (road table, `roadAt`/`flightAt`, pads, `basePad`, `padOrder`), cached per level. `simOpts.level` picks the map (default `LEVEL_0`); the snapshot carries `level`, and the renderer, battlefield, machinery, cables (`cableRoutes(level)`) and layout clearance (`layout(…, level)`) follow it. `defs.ts` keeps level 0 under the old names for dev pages and tests. Per-level sizes: `laneSpread`, `bossRadius` (size only), `turretR` (deck, turret reach `baseR`, outpost art `deckScale`); `narrowed(level)` applies `NARROW_ROAD` (8-unit road, ±1.5 lanes, boss 4, deck 8) for generated levels, while level 0 keeps its painted sizes. Dev: `/dev/towerdefense.html?level=narrow|mirror`; `scripts/td-balance.ts --level=narrow`.
- Generator (`sim/generator.ts`): `generateLevel(seed, { style, pads })` draws a zigzag/trident/lasso/ring/switchback/crossover road (crossover: two bridges) or a two-route fork/merge (`TdLevel.lanes`: walkers take the routes in turn, `splitRoadHp`; `culverts` let cables under a road; geometry `lanes`/`roads`) from straight runs and arcs (mirrored half the time), places the asked pads (6–16, clamped per style by `STYLE_FIT`; ≥ `minPads`) by annealing over individually valid grid spots, and retries until `validateLevel` passes (length 230–335, self-clearance ≥ 13 away from bridges, pads off the road/deck, in the HUD-safe box, cable-reachable; cables may pass under bridge decks) with its style's coverage bar (`STYLE_FIT`: mostly Flamer reach ≥ 88 %; switchback ~0.6, crossover ~0.76 by design). Own seeded rng, ~0.03–0.75 s per level. `planLevel` draws every attempt's seed up front and `runAttempt` runs one, so attempts are independent; `generateLevel` runs them in order, and `render/levelPool.ts` (`generateParallel`) runs them on up to 4 workers and settles on the same map. Maps live in `sim/levelCache.ts` (`levelFor`: baked, cached, else generated; the spec names it); the renderer prefetches the snapshot's `nextQuick` into it, so Quick Ride, its Next level and Endless (`endlessMap(save.endlessSeed)`, a new generated map per run unless `simOpts.level`) start without generating; their ride-select cards preview the built maps. Gate: `scripts/td-level-gate.ts`; dev: `/dev/towerdefense.html?gen=<seed>&style=<style>`.
- Ground kits (`render/ground/`): levels without a painting draw their style's kit from `public/td/kits/<style>/` (WebP + `kit.json`, packed by `scripts/td-pack-kits.ts` from the PNG masters on `td-ground-kits-winners`). `compose.ts` (pure, tested) bombs the ground materials, hive creep and gravel, fits the kit's road strip to the level's widths and stamps clearings and the apron; `bake.ts` runs it in ~6 ms slices over the visible world box at 6–10 px/unit (vector ground meanwhile, ~2.5 µs/px) and adds decals, props, hive mouth and deck shadows. Bridge decks (`deck.ts`: the later pass rides on top) draw over the aliens; walkers underneath show as a dashed brood-coloured outline, and those on the deck are drawn again above it. Dev: `?gen=<seed>&style=&ground=<kit>`.
- Levels as workouts (`sim/campaign.ts`): a `LevelSpec` (seed, minutes, road/ground style, pads, depth) → `levelWaves` (segments: warm-up, sets of 2–3 build/peak segments, bosses closing the last sets, troughs between; sized by `WAVE_MIN` estimates). `CAMPAIGN` = 5 levels × 6 ground styles (15–40 min, 8–14 pads, each ground rotating the road styles; each unlocks the next); `quickRide(seed, minutes)`. The sim swaps maps per run (`useLevel`), ramps a level's difficulty wave from 1 to `levelPeakWave` + depth over its length (`dw`), ends after the last wave (`levelCleared`, stars from base HP lost over the level, heals not counted, `starTwoHp`/`starThreeHp`) and shows Next level / Finish; a fall offers Retry. Intensity (`intensityEasy/Hard`, `…Hp`) scales every tier's rated power (the gearbox stiffens on Hard) and alien HP. The ride select has Campaign / Quick Ride / Endless cards plus Intensity and Stars rows; A on Campaign opens the level grid (`render/levelSelect.ts`: one row of five landscape-shaped art tiles per ground, sized by container units to fill the panel, with the selected level's preview above (portrait) or beside it (landscape, where menus are up to 980u wide); ◀▶ level, ▲▼ ground, A ride, B back; cleared levels replay, locked ones preview). Campaign maps are baked (`sim/campaignLevels.json`, `npx tsx scripts/td-bake-campaign.ts`; which also removes the level-start hitch. New riders start Campaign I. Levels pay catch-up scrap (`levelCatchUpScrap` + `levelCatchUpPerWave` × wave) for each Endless wave their difficulty skips, so deep levels keep pace with Endless income. Star unlocks (`sim/unlocks.ts`): Mortar 4★, tower Lv 4 8★, Hard 20★, Lv 5 30★; Quick Ride grounds open with the campaign. Every tower kind is open from the start (each counters a brood colour). `simOpts.play`, `intensity`; dev `?campaign=<i>` / `?quick=<seed>&minutes=`.
- Menu art and level loads (`render/levelArt.ts`): ride-select cards and campaign tiles show each level's real kit ground, pads and outpost (tiles baked at 2 px/unit in the background, nearest the selection first; big previews cut from the full-screen bake), cached as object URLs for the page's life. In the menu the renderer bakes the level A would start (`Battlefield.prepare`, `GroundBaker` keeps 3 bakes), so it appears at once. The renderer sends `loadGate`; then each new level/run holds (sim `loading`: no time, neutral trainer, input dropped) until `levelReady`, capped by `levelLoadMaxS`; meanwhile the menu frame stays under a loading card (`loadingHtml`). Headless runs never gate. Every visit (new riders too) opens on the ride select. Touch: the Quick Ride card (length; ⟳ New map = `quickReroll`, Y on the controller) and the level picture carry ◀ ▶ buttons (`garageStep {row, step}`, `levelTile`) and step on a sideways swipe (`swipeStep`); the Campaign card opens the grid instead; narrow screens show landscape-shaped card pictures.
- Single road (#163, level 0): `sim/road.ts` is level 0's geometry source (spline road from the hive mouth, 12 pads, Glider flight spawns, paint boxes; pads ≥ `PAINTED_PADS` get a drawn plate in `render/battlefield.ts` until repainted); `sim/defs.ts` derives `roadAt`/`flightAt`/`PADS`/`BASE_PAD`. Gliders fly straight to the turret. `waves.ts`: Endless sets of 3 build segments, a boss peak, a trough; brood order, packs, forecast counts, trough heal.
- `sim/boss.ts`: one boss per colour (Swarm/Ram/Bulwark Tyrant) holds its brood's weapon and trainer mode all fight; HP-gated phases escalate its trick (packs, charges, shield plates; `boss*` tunables); boss waves avoid the last boss's colour (`nextBrood` `avoid`). #140's stagger/Exposed timing stays. Live matchups and fuel-blooded ignition retain turret/tower attribution.
- `sim/targeting.ts`: soonest-arriving brood-colour target; ◀▶/paddles step a decaying lock closer/further along the queue. Turret reach `turretRange` 75 (Flamer: `flameRange`, ground only) leaves the upper road to towers. 
- `sim/construction.ts`: affordable build/upgrade/repair orders use off-colour leak danger consistently for resistance filtering and scoring. Build and upgrade orders quote/pay all funded levels; new-pad ranking keeps basic coverage economical. Manual workshop builds remain Lv 1.
- `sim/types.ts`: snapshot `flow` (stage warm-up/build/peak/boss/trough, set, sets, leftS), `radar`, `set`, `lock`, `burns`, `inputEpoch`; workshop adds `revision`.
- The shell owns cooldown/charge indicators with a controller and floating tappable actions in touch mode; Rail counts and boss sprint windows publish typed indicator data. `render/layout.ts` reclaims the canvas dock space under `mount.hudControls`; bare renderers retain a fallback dock. The radial workshop stays above the actual inset.
- HUD (#126): `render/panels.ts` keeps the shell font but wears the game theme (`render/theme.ts`: riveted gunmetal plates, bronze rims, brass headings/selection/bars, brood-tinted accents; one palette for the DOM CSS and the canvas workshop/dock in `construction.ts`). Shell-owned HUD (bottom bar, action chips, touch keys) is untouched for wave/progress, HP/cores, scrap, current brood/mode and next forecast. No weapon list or duplicate power/controller HUD. Recovery replaces the forecast with a countdown/recover-or-ramp cue in the same brood strip, including while the workshop is open; no centered recovery card. Layout tests cover shell and standalone modes vs insets.
- `render/aliens/` (#132): cut-out puppets with distance-based gaits, cached device-pixel frames, live boss poses, husks, brood splats and fire bursts; vector enemies remain the loading fallback. Assets: `public/td/aliens/`, packed by `scripts/td-pack-aliens.ts`. Battlefield, machinery, conduits, hive entries, loading gate, portrait margins: `render/NOTES.md`.
- Sound (#150): `render/dynamoSound.ts` maps cadence, power and weapon to glided synth layers (`src/audio/voices.ts`): hum on cadence with a per-stroke swell, growl/sub on power, Gatling whine, fireball-furnace roar on watts, rail whine, octave-up Overdrive. TD one-shots are new `sfx.ts` recipes. Demo `dev/td-sound.html`. #151: `render/overdrive.ts` (hot cables, muzzle, edge shimmer, payoff rings, alien flinch; sim `payoff` fx). #152: `render/hitFeedback.ts` (per-enemy `hitCls` → strong sprays / resisted ricochets, heavy-only damage numbers, chain and multikill callouts; rate-limited). #153: `aliens/gibs.ts` (heavy deaths throw real parts; husks skip them; the boss comes apart), boss name card on `bossIntro`, Exposed glow and first-hit slow-down, stagger pose. Demo `dev/td-gibs.html`. #155: `render/structureFx.ts` (build/upgrade parts drop in on sim time, landing dust, level pips with a flash; base sparks <75%, steam <50%, beacons + alarm <25%).

## Play and controls (#119, #120, #122, #123)
- Continuous flow (phase 3): a level is one stream in segments ("waves" in code) that roll straight into each other: build `segmentS` 120 (a set ramps from `buildPressure` 0.75 to its peak), boss peak (ends when the boss dies, `bossPeakMaxS` cap), trough `troughS` 100 (half-rate drones; a clean trough heals `troughHeal` 30, leaks scale it to 0 at `leakHealZero`). Only a fresh run has a quiet start (`firstLullS` 15). Nothing waits for the field to clear; the last segment waits up to 45 s for it before the level is done. Endless ramps `endlessRamp` 0.7 difficulty waves per segment. Called efforts (spin/grind/hold, Overclock, EMP) are gone. Radar (`radarView`, every 0.25 s): incoming HP per colour in 10 s buckets over `radarS` 90 by time to the base, Glider counts, boss marker, `lead`; `scale` a decaying peak. HUD: horizontal radar in the brood strip (portrait/phones), upright in the landscape objective card; status shows Set n/N · stage.
- Controls (continuous-combat phase 2): Y / B / Z mount Gatling / Fireball / Railgun (`swapS` 3 s, turret silent; works with the menu open). Riders keep their pick; `opts.weapon: 'auto'` (default with autopilot) lets bots switch to the weapon whose matchups pay most against the radar (the boss's counter in a boss peak). The D-pad moves a pad cursor (`padStep`: nearest pad/base within 60° of the on-screen direction; landscape turns it), A opens the build menu there. Paddles step the target lock.
- Building is manual, any time; troughs are the natural moment. The bot spends as each segment starts. The workshop opens on the pad the headless bot would pick and preselects its tower, as a nudge only.
- Build menu: a 2-column grid of options with Exit across the bottom (`menuStep`; row index = rows.length is Exit). D-pad moves, A confirms (A on Exit closes), 10 s idle timeout. Tap pads/base to open, tap rows/Exit. Touch and controller share selection and purchase state.
- Intensity tiers (`sim/tiers.ts`, continuous-combat redesign phase 1): the shifters (either hood) and the HUD − / + step tier I–V (`tierUp`/`tierDown`). The trainer stays in resistance mode all run: the game is the gearbox. Each tier asks for the level that makes its rated power (`tier1Ftp`…`tier5Ftp`, 0.45–1.05 × intensity) at `gearboxRefRpm` 85, from the trainer response learned from live watts (`sim/fireballLevel.ts`, #282; prior `gearboxPriorLevelPct`), eased over `gearboxShiftS` and relieved below `gearboxReliefRpm`. Output is the tier's shape × `effortFactor` (watts / rated, cap `tierEffortCap`): Gatling pellets (cadence = rate; tier = damage, pierce, splash), Fireball (cadence = lob beat; tier = radius, burn time, burn share), Railgun pulses (bank `railPulseS` of rated watts; tier = damage, width, pierce; light aliens get a quick snap that spends only what it needs, `railSnapMinShare`/`railSnapS`). `suggestedTier` (warm-up I, trough II, build III, peak/boss IV, boss openings V) feeds the card, zone target and `hooks.powerFtp`. Headless: `simOpts.tier` (number or 'auto', default auto with autopilot); script `follow-power` rides the suggestion.
- Fireball resistance (`sim/fireballLevel.ts`): the level is chosen for a power, not fixed. `weaponDemand()` → level via a learned k (ftpFraction per level % at `flameCueRpm`, power ~ rpm^`flameRpmExponent`), prior `flameLevelPct` 18 at `flameFtpFraction` 0.85; learned from settled samples (τ `flameCalTauS`), capped at `flameMaxLevelPct` and 4x around the prior.
- Combat inputs are consumed while construction is open. Stale menu revisions/epochs/quotes and same-tick touch/controller duplicates cannot buy twice. The shell sends `inputCancel` on pause to discard queued input and resume edges.
- Mortar (4th tower, neutral: `TOWER_WEAPON.mortar = null`, `towerMatch()` → x1 vs every brood): long-range ground-only splash shells that lead the target (`mortarFlightS`, `mortarSplashMult`) and concuss what they hit (`mortarDazeS`/`mortarDazeSlow`, not the boss; `EnemyView.dazed`). Its hits never ignite. Deliberately low DPS; the build bot credits it only `NEUTRAL_SHARE` of the off-colour push, so it is filler, never preferred over a strong specialist (test). Mortar rounds are `ShellView.tower` shells from their pad.
- Flamer lobs fireballs (burst + burning road, `burnShare`); the Flame Tower uses approved compact-burner art and a short ground-only cone. ◀▶/paddles step the target lock; ▲ releases it. Repair remains a scrap purchase; cooldown Field Repair is deferred by owner choice.

## Saves and balance
- Save v4 (`readSave` in `sim/sim.ts`): runs, campaign stars, intensity, Quick Ride choice, `endlessBest` and lifetime totals. Parts, bank, upgrades, Forward Base and the old best wave are dropped; a save that bought upgrades is marked `veteran` (unlocks up to 12★). A fall or bail returns to the ride select; `isOver()` stays false. Tests use `simOpts.stars` and `simOpts.startWave` in place of Garage progress.
- Overcharge and Slow field are gone (owner, 2026-11-18): sprint Overdrive is the effort trigger, the Mortar's concussion is the slow. Their 4★/8★ rungs now unlock the Mortar (4★) and Tower Lv 4 (8★). `botBuild` (default with `autopilot`) is the headless build bot; riders always build by hand.
- The `surge-ready-deep` achievement fixture uses hook-responsive riding and a reinforced base (`simOpts.baseHp`) to reach deep waves. `wavesSurvived` is the best single run, so a later fall does not erase it. Passive survival remains measured separately. In Fireball/Railgun waves the towers the brood does not resist answer to how close the rider is to the suggested tier's power, so a steady 0.65 FTP ride leaks on Hard (Campaign I: 2★) and following the suggestion does not.
- #156: 30 Hive Tyrants, 8,000 strong-matchup kills; the sim also reports `bestMaxedTowers` and emits `towerSold`. Summary keys: `bestChain`, `strongKills`, `exposedSprinted`, `spotlessWaves` (segments without a leak), `bestRunSpotless`, `weaponSwitches`, `highTierSeconds`; `bossKilled.topGear` (never under tier V while it lived). Turret share of damage is ~0.3 for a zone-2 bot now that towers always have work (phase 5 balance). Ignition caps at 6; Kindling is a chain of 3 and Bonfire is 4.
- Balance: wave planning and swarm pacing have independent seeded RNG streams, so combat/ignition no longer reroll future broods. Funded new builds avoid the Lv-1 hoarding bottleneck; `towerDamageMult` 0.85 restores endurance pacing. Reproduce first-run seed sweeps with `node --import tsx scripts/td-balance.ts`; methods, metrics and limits: [balance report](../../../docs/towerdefense/BALANCE.md).

## Achievement curve
Triple Rail requires 25 pulses that hit 3+. Exposed! counts five defeated bosses that were
individually sprinted during an Exposed window. Rules v4: Surge Protector, Spin Cycle, On Call and
Iron Lungs are retired with their bonus XP; their rewards moved to Quick Change (200 switches),
Top Gear (a boss killed without leaving tier V), Redline (120 min at tier IV+) and Long Haul (clear a
level after 30+ active minutes). Levels (rules v3): the wave-depth,
Spotless and Full Workshop achievements are retired and revoked with their bonus XP;
their rewards moved to Clear 5/15/30 levels, 3★ on 10 levels, Hard Line and Double Shift
(summary `campaignCleared`/`campaignThreeStars` from the save; `levelCleared` events).
