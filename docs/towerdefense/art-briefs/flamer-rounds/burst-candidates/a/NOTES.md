# Gold Folds

Long gold folds carry the light from the duckbill slot through a broad fan. Dark vermilion troughs separate the folds, and small amber slivers peel off the scalloped leading edge. Two overlapping, outward-moving noise fields keep the folds rolling through the loop boundary.

At 20 fps, play all six start frames, all twelve sustain frames, sustain 00–03 again, then all six end frames for a 1.4-second burst. Align the left edge at y = 150 with the center of the mouth. The sheet reads left to right, then down.

Run `python3 paint.py` from the parent folder to regenerate both studies with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow.
