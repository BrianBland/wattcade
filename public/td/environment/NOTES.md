# Dynamo Defense environment

Single-road terrain uses road/portrait.webp and the optional road/landscape.webp,
registered by PAINT_BOXES in sim/road.ts. Sources and measurements: road/NOTES.md.
The live alien renderer loads only parts/splat_1.png through parts/splat_6.png.
Unused burrow/creep/prop overlays, glow duplicates, their old manifest and generated
preview were removed. The painted terrain already supplies those features.
Material references required for the landscape painting remain under
docs/towerdefense/art-reference/environment/.
FILES.txt and SHA256SUMS.txt cover this complete production directory.
