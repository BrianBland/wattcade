# B · Crosswind

Broader sheets of fire lift, fold and peel sideways. Variant 0 sweeps right, 1 sends a long tongue left, 2 stays short and wide, and 3 has a high back tongue that curls over its shorter neighbor. The sides cool to red-orange while the brightest fuel stays at each foot.

Anchor the 64×128 tufts at (32, 120) and offset their 16-frame loops at 18 fps. Center the eight-frame flare under the Fireball impact and play it once at 20 fps. Short, slanting flames travel around the patch and leave gaps as the front burns out.

Play the corresponding eight-frame dying strip once, starting from the loop’s 00 pose. At 18 fps, a thread of smoke and a few embers outlast the flame over roughly 0.44 seconds.

The eight-tuft sheet preview uses the game’s volcanic road. All effect frames are straight-alpha sprites. Run `python3 -B render.py` from `groundfire/` to regenerate the PNGs and both sheets.
