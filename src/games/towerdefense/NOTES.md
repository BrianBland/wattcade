# Dynamo Defense (towerdefense)

CONTRACT-REQUESTs: none.

## Layout
- `sim/defs.ts` (pure): enemy kinds, towers + level tables, 3 curved Bézier lanes (`laneAt` arc-length lookup), 6 build pads, base geometry. Aim angle 0 = up from the base.
- `sim/waves.ts` (pure, rng passed in): `nextBrood` (brood colour order: no colour > `broodMaxRun` in a row, all three in every `broodCoverWaves`), `planWave` (`broodShare` of the wave's colour slots in the brood colour, the rest off-colour for the towers; red/purple slots are heavies, blue slots are swarm packs of `swarmPackSize` darts or drones; lane bias, dart packs, prisms, boss every 5); per-lane colour counts feed the forecast.
- `sim/targeting.ts` (pure): `chooseTarget` (lead brood-colour alien on the most threatened lane, nudge bias, stickiness), `laneThreats`.
- `sim/boss.ts` (pure): HP phase gates, announced countdowns, stagger, telegraph and Exposed clocks.
- `sim/efforts.ts` (pure): seeded call spacing and cadence/power qualification.
- `sim/meta.ts`: Garage upgrades (cost = base x growth^level), `metaMods`, `autoInvest`, `partsForWave`.
- `sim/sim.ts`: `createTdSim(ctx, { autopilot?, skipGarage?, cores? })`, `readSave` (migrates the v1 `{ bestWave }` save). `sim/types.ts`: `TdSnapshot` view-model.
- `render/`: `layout.ts` (pure; wide = side panels, narrow = top bar; tested vs insets), `renderer.ts` (cached background, particles, fx, shake/flash, cues -> `audio/sfx`, ambient flame/hum, taps), `sprites.ts` (procedural enemies, towers, dynamo base + turret), `panels.ts` (weapons/rail/dynamo, wave/base/surge/forecast/legend, workshop, garage, banners).

## Rules (all numbers are `towerdefense.*` tunables)
- Brood waves (#119): each wave is one colour; the turret mounts its weapon for the whole wave (no manual or per-enemy switching): Gatling = sim +1% (bullets per crank rev), Flamer = resistance 35% (dps per watt), Railgun = ERG 0.95 FTP (laser). Rail charges still build at >= 0.75 FTP in any wave (max 3; Z tap = 1, hold 0.5 s = all).
- Lull (`lullS` 15 s) before every wave, wave 1 included: no spawns, brood card + countdown (`snapshot.brood`, `nextBrood` for the forecast). The weapon and its trainer mode switch at the lull's start at a recovery value (`lull*`), ramping to full over the last `rampS` (6 s). Event `broodAnnounced`; `waveStart` fires when the lull ends.
- Turret (#120): always aims itself (`chooseTarget`; lane threat = sum of dmg x (0.3 + progress)^2); falls back to any colour so it never idles. The swarm stays blue (towers' job in red/purple waves).
- Broods (#134): mini/dart = crystalline, bioluminescent chitin; brute/charger = fuel-blooded with volatile bladders; bastion/carrier = sealed armour plates. Prism cycles colour every 4 s from wave 12. `MATCHUP[weapon][element]` supplies nine live `match_<weapon>_<element>` tunables: Gatling = blue ×1.5, red ×1, purple ×0.5; Flamer = blue ×0.5, red ×1.5, purple ×1; Rail = blue ×1, red ×0.5, purple ×1.5. Tesla/Mortar/Lens share those rows. Prism and gold bosses use their current colour.
- Ignition (#134): a red alien killed by fire has `ignitionChance` (0.6) to burst for `ignitionDamage` (20) fire damage within `ignitionRadius` (10), only against nearby reds. All descendants share an `ignitionChainCap` (3) burst budget. `burst` events and snapshot fx drive the renderer; effective damage and kills retain the original turret/tower attribution. Workshop suggestions cover the next brood's largest off-colour push with its strongest safe tower.
- Boss ladder (#125): every fifth wave has `bossPhases` (3; tunable 2–3) distinct colours, starting with a weapon different from the preceding wave. Phase HP gates cannot be skipped by overkill: at each boundary the next colour, weapon and trainer mode are announced for `bossPhaseCountdownS` (3 s). Only then does the weapon change, with four escorts staggered 0.3 s apart and a 0.6 s boss stagger. `snapshot.boss` and enemy fields expose the ladder and openings. The Goliath art stays. Bosses retreat at wave timeout, so they cannot change a later wave's mode.
- Boss openings: the first is half a cycle after spawn (10 s); subsequent windows repeat every `bossExposedEveryS` (20 s), telegraphed for `bossTelegraphS` (3 s), Exposed for `bossExposedS` (5 s) at ×2 damage with a further ×1.5 if sprinting. The turret prioritizes an Exposed boss in reach. HUD/hooks stop asking for a sprint at an immune phase gate. During a Rail opening, optional hold-A raises the ERG target to `bossSprintFtp` (1.35 FTP); releasing it restores the phase target. This makes a real power sprint possible without another mode switch. Renderer hit-stop is capped at 0.1 s; the sim and trainer continue.
- Stall: under 15 rpm for `stallGraceS` (3 s) the dynamo stalls (`snapshot.stalled`, banner): shields down, leaks hit x`stallDamageMult` (2.2, keeping the idle scenario inside 150 s after the new tower suggestions changed early scrap spending).
- Dynamo: towers fire at `ftpFraction / towerNominalFtp` speed (0.65 FTP = 100%, cap 160%, 0 under 15 rpm). Sprinting = Overdrive (x1.5 damage + tower speed).
- Towers: Tesla (chain, blue), Mortar (splash shells, red), Prism Lens (beam at the toughest, purple); Lv 1-5. Scrap from kills/waves buys, upgrades, sells (50%) and repairs the base.
- Workout rhythm (#124): a call every 2–3 waves (`callGapMinWaves`/`callGapMaxWaves`), never consecutive, skipping bosses. Announce only after the lull, with `callCountdownS` (5 s), then hold the target for `callNeedS` (8 s) within `callWindowS` (18 s). Gatling = spin (Overclock + scrap), Flamer = low-cadence grind at power, Rail = ERG hold (both EMP + scrap + rail charge). None is required to clear a wave. Targets use a 30 s recent cadence/power mean with a Zone 2 power floor; hold temporarily sets ERG to the recent endurance target (56–75% FTP) in the same mode. `snapshot.effort`, power-sourced HUD bands and cadence hints show the exact targets; `hooks.cadence`/`powerFtp` let fake riders answer. Legacy spin/surge summary and achievement events remain; `callsStarted`/`callsWon`/`callsFailed` report all calls. Calls end before recovery and are cancelled as failed if a wave finishes first.
- Reactor cores (lives, `cores` default 3; 0 = unlimited): losing the base clears the field and rebuilds it; the last core ends the run (`isOver`).
- Waves end early when the field is clear after `waveMinS` (the drone stream stops once the wave has sent everything and `waveMinS` has passed), else at `waveMaxS` (60 s). An early clear heals `earlyClearHeal` + up to `speedClearHeal` for speed, scaled down by the base damage leaked that wave (0 at `leakHealZero`; `clearHealFor` in waves.ts).

## Controls (bike-first)
- Z rail; D-pad left/right or paddles = lane nudge (biases targeting for `nudgeS` 6 s, decaying; up cancels); down = workshop. Hold A during a Rail boss telegraph/opening opts into the ERG sprint push; otherwise A/B/Y are free in play (for #122). All four shifters are virtual gears (`useVirtualGears(true)`, live in sim mode = Gatling waves and the garage); no game action reads a shifter. `profile.settings.aimMode` is ignored here.
- Workshop: left/right = pad (6 pads + base repair), up/down = option (preset to the tower the lane's next push needs), A = build/upgrade, B = close (auto-closes after 10 s). Taps: pads, base, rows, rail pips.
- Garage (returning riders only; skipped on the first run and under autopilot): up/down + A buys, left/right sets start wave, B starts; auto-starts after 30 s pedalling.

## Progression / save
- `{ v: 2, bestWave, bank, upgrades, runs, autoInvest, totals }`; parts bank on every wave clear and boss kill, persisted then, at game over and on dispose.
- Balance (autopilot, FTP 200, fresh save, seeds 1–5): 20 min Zone 2 kills four bosses, matching the baseline; 30 min clears about 34 waves, with turret damage inside the existing 40–60% band. Zone 2 wins most calls; hook-responsive wins all, idle none. Checked via brood-z2, z2-economy and workout scenarios. Effective damage includes ignition and excludes overkill/phase-gate damage.
