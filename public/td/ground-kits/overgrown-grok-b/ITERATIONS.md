# overgrown-grok-b-v2 (polish round, Overgrown Ruins, iteration 1 of 2)

Lean: painterly richness. Won Overgrown Ruins in Round 3. Polish: separate the path from the paving more.

## What changed and why
New road: warm grey-tan worn slabs, polished in the middle, chipped, with thin moss in the joints. New ground_a: the same paving but with dark, near-black olive moss packed in the joints so each slab reads separately. The path sat 21 px high and its ends differed by 31 in luminance; shifted it down 22 px and blended the end columns. 

## Checks
PASS. band 234 px, centre +1, wobble 8, seam 2.7, roadVsGround 1.9. ground gradient 0.05, brood 0.

## Self-scores (1–5)
- reads 4: the warm path is clearly lighter than the grey paving, and the dark joints make the surrounding slabs read as stones rather than one texture. Aliens and towers pop.
- style 4: worn, sun-bleached slabs against dark moss, in the lean.
- composites 4: band straight, seam closed by blending the ends, no vignette.
- lighting 4: soft overhead, landscape shows no light direction.
- scale 4: band 234 px, just over the brief's 224.
- variety 4: ground_a is now distinct from the path; the rest carries from v1.

## Weakest point
The path slabs are large, so a curve shows only a few of them. They read as worn stone, which was the ask.
