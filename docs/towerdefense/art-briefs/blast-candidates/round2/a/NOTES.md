# A · Furnace Crown

**Fireball.** The flash opens wide, with the caldera at full radius on frame 02. A continuous gold lip rolls over the saturated orange body while the center hollows. Play once, centered, at 16 fps.

**Mortar.** A two-pose lemon-white flash gives way to one broad pressure crest. Five banks of sunlit soil carry gravel outward. The crest casts a dark inner shadow so it holds together on a pale road. Play once at 20 fps. The loose gravel and torn steel pieces are individual variants on 40 px canvases.

**Ignition.** Three paired vents open along the back of the alien. Dark chitin and burning ichor kick out past the red-orange tongues. The spine clears as the flanks burn down. Play once at 20 fps. The six 16 px ember sprites are individual particles shared by ignition and Fireball.
