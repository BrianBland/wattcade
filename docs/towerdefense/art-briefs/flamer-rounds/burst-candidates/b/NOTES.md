# Copper Roller

A gold throat opens into a copper-red front. Tighter, rounder curls pull bright hooked rims around darker pockets, with fine torn edges and a few trailing embers. The palette follows the incandescent slot on the tower and the molten launcher mouth.

At 20 fps, play start 00–05, sustain 00–11, sustain 00–03, then end 00–05. That gives the front 1.4 seconds to open, roll and draw back. Anchor at (0, 150). The four rows on the sheet show ignition, two rows of sustain and withdrawal.

The shared renderer is `../paint.py` and uses NumPy, SciPy and Pillow.
