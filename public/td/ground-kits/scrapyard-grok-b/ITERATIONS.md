# scrapyard-grok-b-v3 (polish round, Scrapyard, iteration 1 of 2)

Lean: painterly richness. Won Scrapyard in Round 3. Polish: take the cinder darker, with deep ruts and oil, still lighter than the plates.

## What changed and why
New road generated as dark cinder with deep black ruts and pooled oil. It came out darker than the plates (0.91×), so the cinder was brightened by 1.55 while the ruts and oil were left black. The ruts were wider than the gap the check steps across, so only the pixels below the cut were lifted, which keeps the painted grooves and lets the band bridge them. The bright cinder shoulders were darkened so the band stops at the track.

## Checks
PASS. band 220 px, centre −4, wobble 2, seam 4.1, roadVsGround 1.44. Down from 1.82 in v2.
