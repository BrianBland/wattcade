# badlands-grok-c v1 prompts (round 3)

Rules prefix: top-down orthographic, straight down, painterly, crisp, muted, soft overhead light only, no vignette, no long shadows, no text, no saturated blue/red/purple. Ochre and tan, not rust red.

ground_a: ochre and tan sandstone strata, wind-carved rock, cracked clay pans, quiet, mean ~42%.
ground_b: the same sandstone, rougher and darker, more cracked clay and deeper grooves.
gravel: fine sand and small sandstone chips, slightly lighter.
road: a pale caliche path, light limestone grey-tan, soft feathered edge, faint ruts. Rebuilt to a 170 px band so it stays inside the gate.
overlay_hive: dark tar seep with dull brown crust, ragged connected patches, on flat #00FF00. Small detached specks removed and the green halo snapped to exact green.
props: cattle skull, rock arch fragment, mining cart, fossil slab, dead shrub, wooden scaffold, a stone, a timber stake, all top-down on flat #00FF00. Loose stones around the arch removed.
decals: stone-and-timber apron, pale caliche clearing, six flat decals (tar, salt crust, cracks, wagon tracks, tar smear, scuff). Green halo snapped to exact green.
bridge: rope-and-timber trestle deck, two end caps, one rope rail, on flat #00FF00.
hive_mouth: one sinkhole in sandstone, dark centre, on flat #00FF00.
