# Gold Jet · round 3

A turbulent yellow-white throat feeds a saturated orange fan. The axis carries the densest fire. Farther out, individual tongues carry more pigment and opacity, and gaps between them reveal the road and targets. A broken yellow-orange rim rolls over the red tips as the burst advances and withdraws. Soft orange light bleeds beyond the rim.

The tongues form where overlapping bands of fuel shear, split and rejoin. Their pattern travels 180 px per 0.6-second cycle (300 px/s). The rim curves around eddies of different sizes and follows the current reach of the jet.

At 20 fps, play start 00–05, sustain 00–11, sustain 00–03, then end 00–05 for a 1.4-second burst. Align (0, 150) to the mouth. Frames use straight alpha. The sheet reads left to right, then down.

Run `python3 paint.py` from the parent folder to regenerate both studies with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow.
