# scrapyard-grok-b-v1

Round 1, iteration 1. Lean: painterly richness.

Prompts (prefixed with the brief rules: top-down, soft overhead light, muted palette):
- ground_a: rusted steel plates and riveted grating, chipped corners, oil-black dirt in the gaps, dull rivets, rough brushwork. Rust brown, not orange.
- ground_b: the same, rougher, wider gaps, torn grating, heavier dull rust, scattered evenly.
- gravel: metal shavings, bolts and cinder, finer and lighter than the plates.
- road: compacted grey-tan cinder, even band, embedded bolts, edges fading into loose cinder, no ruts, no outline. Rusted plates above and below.
- props: crates, oil drum, pipe bundle, gear, cable reel, crane hook, scrap heap, dead generator, on flat #00FF00.
- decals: 160 px steel floor plate, 420 px riveted brass-and-steel apron, six decals (oil, dull rust run-off, welding scorch, puddle, smear, bolts).
- bridge: checker-plate deck on girders, two end caps, one railing.
- hive_mouth: torn-open storm drain, dark centre.
- overlay_hive: dull rust-brown corrosion bloom with dark oily seep, ragged edges.

The first road had tyre ruts darker than the shoulders, so the gate's window slid off centre. Regenerated as one even cinder band, shifted it onto the centre line, and darkened the steel plates beside it so the cinder stays the brightest band. Green key snapped to pure #00FF00. The gear's centre hole and a red rust streak were regenerated out.
