Systems in one glance

Fishing (the core loop)

  • Pick a spot: paddles move a marker between 2–3 spots (rocks / kelp / pier end). Each spot has a different fish mix.
  • Cast: press A. Cadence at the moment of release sets the distance (cast farther for bigger fish).
  • Wait: light pedaling turns the reel and the lure drifts. Spinning a bit faster makes it twitch, which brings more bites. No penalty.
  • Bite: the bobber plunges. Press A within a generous 2 s window (it auto-sets after 3 s, at half credit).
  • Pull: a short sprint. The fish's stamina drains while you're above its target (110–150% FTP × 6–25 s, scaled by size). Trainer resistance surges when the fish runs.
  • Fail only if you land under ~60% of the target. The fish wins, a funny line plays, and you recast.

Economy (catches matter, gently)

  • Fish sell by weight at Lou's. Rods: Pier Rod (start) → Surf Rod (shop 1) → "the Deep" (pier stand), which is needed to hook the Big One.
  • A rider who lands most of the bites has enough money with a margin. If you're short, a bonus turnout appears (+3 min of fishing) before the next shop. You're never stuck.
  • Extra money buys flair: lures, a bucket hat, a nicer bobber, a bell for the bars. Some of these show up again later (the hubcap and the old rod in the belly).

Drilling

  • ERG at Z3, low cadence (60–75 rpm target). Each material is a layer with its own ERG watts, colour, and sound.
  • Progress = depth. Cadence in the target band adds a bonus to the bore rate. Drifting off-band just slows the bore; you can't fail.
  • Ten-second easy "clear the chips" breaks between layers act as micro-rests.

Movement

  • Paddles steer left/right inside the shoulder or path width. The camera rails along an authored spline.
  • Scenes are authored in distance. Virtual speed is nudged toward the 2 h target, so riding harder feels faster without breaking the edit.
  • Fishing, drilling and the fan are effort-gated, not time-gated.

Chapters

  • Shown only as Morning / Midday / Evening. Deliberately vague; each ends on a quiet beat (sinking, the temple hall, the credits).
  • Each chapter opens soft, so the opening scene doubles as a warm-up. Progress saves at chapter boundaries; chapter select unlocks after the first full ride.

Sound

  • Ambient beds per area (surf + gulls + tyres on asphalt, gravel crunch, Lou's radio, wet heartbeat, jungle insects, cave drips, lava hiss and boom, silence in the fall, roaring fire on re-entry, wind under the wings).
  • Stock CC music only on a few cues: title, the Big One, the island reveal, the Earth growing, the final lift-off and credits. CC-BY attribution goes in the credits roll.

Monologue

  • Speech bubble at the top-left, one line, ≤ 8 words, 4 s, and never during a sprint.
  • The voice is dry and accepting. It gets calmer as things get stranger. Silence is used on purpose (the fall, re-entry, the final shot).

A loop hint that stays mundane

In the first two minutes a large white "bird" glides west, far out over the water, once (panel 02). The thought: "Big bird." At the end you're that shape over the same water (panel 32). Nothing in between mentions it.
