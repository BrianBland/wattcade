# B · Split Crown

**Fireball.** Three vents interrupt the caldera’s bright rim and open a wider view through the middle. The opening flash reaches out over the first three poses. Play the ten frames once at 16 fps.

**Mortar.** A broad pressure wave with a broken inner lip runs ahead of seven shorter fans of ochre dirt. The two-pose flash picks up the tower’s hazard yellow. Play once at 20 fps. The gravel has pale fractured faces and the casing pieces have a sheared steel edge.

**Ignition.** The armor opens on a diagonal, throwing longer, hooked red flames off the rear plates. Small hot seams flicker out first, leaving gaps between the flanks for neighboring targets to show through. Play once at 20 fps. Pick from the six ember and spark sprites for loose particles on either fire hit.

Pair with round 1 A’s ball, trail, shell and craters. Run `python3 -B render.py` from `round2/` with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow 10.1 or later.
