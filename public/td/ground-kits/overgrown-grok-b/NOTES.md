# overgrown-grok-b-v1

Round 3, iteration 1. Lean: painterly richness.

Prompts (prefixed with the brief rules: top-down, soft overhead light, muted palette):
- ground_a: broken ancient paving, chunky slabs with crisp rims, muted olive moss and ferns in the cracks. Regenerated once to remove a spiral of roots that would have repeated when bombed.
- ground_b: the same, rougher, wider cracks, heavier moss, exposed roots, darker damp patches scattered evenly.
- gravel: mossy rubble and leaf litter, finer than the paving.
- road: worn pale stone path, moss in the joints, a few polished ruts, pebbles fading into the paving.
- props: mossy stone, pot shard, root tangle, fallen pillar, statue head, urn, mossy boulder, broken arch, on flat #FF00FF.
- decals: 160 px mosaic floor, 420 px carved stone apron (its centre was filled in; it generated as a hollow ring), moss, puddle, crack, leaves, moss, stones.
- bridge: stone slab deck, two end caps, one railing.
- hive_mouth: cracked crypt opening, dark centre.
- overlay_hive: dark thorny bramble with grey-black growths. Magenta spill in the thorns was pulled toward grey.

Stamps use magenta because the style is green. Road shifted down 26 px to centre, and the darker joints lifted so the band holds across every column.
