# B · Caldera & Quarry

**Fireball.** A compact, deeper red globe has narrow incandescent seams and two flame streams that separate behind it. The blast vents through three large lobes. An opening clears in the middle while the outer folds are still bright, keeping the target visible through the crown.

**Mortar.** A longer iron shell tumbles end over end with twin yellow driving bands and a short fuse. A double pressure crest leads the cooler quarry dust. Pale grit catches the light against the dark earth ring, then settles. The three crater decals have grey broken rims scattered with angular chips.

Anchor the ball at (112, 32) over the tail. Both flight strips loop independently at 16 fps. The shell also loops. Center impacts on contact and play them once at 16 fps for fireball or 20 fps for mortar. Choose one crater per hit. The gravel and torn casing pieces are individual debris sprites.

Run `python3 paint.py` from the parent folder to regenerate the original art and sheets with NumPy, SciPy and Pillow (10.1 or later).
