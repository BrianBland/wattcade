# warmup — Warm-up Ride (lobby)
CONTRACT-REQUEST: none.
The free ride the shell runs behind its menus (`lobbyGame` in `games/registry.ts`; not in the picker,
but in `allGames` so `npm run sim:all` runs its scenarios). Never ends.
## Sim (`sim/`)
- `terrain.ts` (pure, seeded): `makeTerrain(seed, maxGrade)` -> `grade(d)` % and `elev(d)` m (analytic integral of grade, three interfering waves, |grade| <= maxGrade). `biomeAt(d, biomeM, start, blendM)` loops `BIOMES`; a `blendM` (tunable `warmup.blendM`, 300) window centred on each boundary dissolves `cur -> next` (`mix`), `dom` flips at the centre; `biomeWeights()` gives each biome's share.
- `sim.ts`: grade under the wheels = `hill * terrain.grade(d)`, sent as ResistanceEffect `terrain` (sim mode), virtual gears on. On-screen speed from `bike.raw.power` with a simple drag + rolling + slope model (`physics.ts`). Emits `biomeReached`.
- `traffic.ts`: occasional same-direction riders from other local profiles (identity + resolved equipped look only). An Easter egg: first encounter after 5 min, then one opportunity every 5 min, with no duplicate active profile. Pace uses the current player's 55% FTP Z1/Z2 boundary, mass and road physics, including grade at each peer's position. Faster players catch riders entering ahead; slower/stopped players are passed by riders entering behind. Viewport bounds keep spawning/culling offscreen; speed-matched riders fade out after 2 min so encounters keep rotating. `receive({type:'riders', riders})` refreshes the roster/cosmetics and removes deleted profiles without restarting the session; `receive({type:'riderView', view})` supplies the visible road range. Traffic RNG is separate from the player's ride.
- opts: `startDistance`, `startBiome`, `terrainSeed` (the shell passes them on restart so the road doesn't jump), optional `riders` roster.
- Tunables (`warmup.*`): `hills` (bool; off fades the road and grade to flat over `hillFadeS`), `maxGrade`, `exaggeration` (screen hill height vs trainer grade), `biomeKm`, `cda`, `crr`, `hillFadeS`.
- hud(): target Z1-Z2 "Warm-up", gears; persistent controller-only legend retains D-pad Move / A Select / B Back, but `touchControls: []` delegates touch navigation to native arcade menus.
## Render (`render/`)
- Canvas 2D, low-res buffer (~230 px tall) upscaled without smoothing; text and the grade chip / road-profile panel drawn at full res on top. Layers: sky (+stars), celestial, clouds, backdrop (parallax .05), a ridge that is the real road profile zoomed out (4 m/px), mid ground + props (.5), road + ground (1, drawn with the same exaggerated elev), rider (tilted by the drawn slope), foreground bits (1.35), actors (birds, gulls, UFOs with tractor beams).
- Biomes (loop): countryside, seaside, underwater, jungle, desert, volcano, snow, moon, autumn, marsh, market, sky. New IDs are appended to retain old start indices and achievement bits. `paint.ts` = helpers + `BiomeLook` (colours, backdrop, mid/fore props, celestial, actor kind, particles, rider gear, outdoor, ambient bed); `biomes.ts` (first three + `LOOKS`), `biomesMore.ts` (the rest). Everything cross-dissolves by `biomeWeights` (colours, sky, celestials, particles, ambient bed); layers (backdrop/mid/fore) are drawn per biome into a scratch buffer and composited with `blend.ts layerAlphas`. A gateway landmark (`gateway.ts`, styled by destination biome) stands at each boundary. Add a biome = `BIOMES` + `BIOME_NAMES` (terrain.ts) + a look in `LOOKS`.
- `biomesNew.ts`: approved woodland, marsh, market and sky art; continuous-grade road structures, swaying reeds/lanterns, stateless alternating frog hops with landing ripples, floating gardens and pinwheels. Sky actors include a slow whale and birds; actor RNG is separate from simulation RNG.
- `worldPolish.ts`: all twelve looks gain larger road-anchored scenery groups, branching trees, detailed landmarks and revised backdrops. `roadside` hooks use world-pixel scroll and the actual road-height function; draw before the road/rider and composite sparse layers by biome weight. Floating sky terrain sets `openSky` to suppress solid ridge/mid-ground. Tree geometry scales together so small distant trees retain correct proportions. Round-4 renders, plan, actual before/after captures and motion proof are in `docs/visual-polish/biomes/README.md`.
- Sky underside: shared `skyRock.ts` builds overlapping cliff fragments with unequal tips, broken shelves and a loose overall triangular taper. Broad faces stop at different depths; avoid radial crystal facets, a mathematically trimmed cone, repeated teeth or column strips. Seeded lean/depth variation, darker near cliffs, muted far cliffs, draping sod and partial cloud occlusion support the landscape reading. Applies to distant gardens and road undersides. Round-7 stills, seeded variants and gameplay clip: `docs/visual-polish/biomes/README.md`. Road, arches and grade are unchanged.
- Actors per biome (birds, gulls, parrots, vultures, fish schools + jellyfish, lava bombs, UFOs); a biome change sends the old ones away. Particles are stateless (index + time): snow, ash, bubbles, fireflies, dust.
- Day/night (`daylight.ts`, tested) for outdoor biomes from the real clock: night sky, moon, stars, headlight, sunset tint. `?hour=21` previews a time. No looping noise bed (it read as constant static). Scenery is the one-shots in `soundscape.ts`; the jungle falls voice only plays while that cliff is on screen.
- `rider.ts`: procedural rider (profile jersey colour, two-bone legs from `bike.crankRevs`; bubble helmet on the Moon, scuba gear underwater).
- Local riders draw at 82% scale in the farther lane, before the player's silhouette, with their names and biome gear. Renderer sends its visible road range through `mount.send`, including when a fresh lobby session resets its view.
- The shared rider uses a binary-alpha solid-art pass plus separate translucent gear/effects. A bounded one-tick cadence extrapolation smooths fixed-rate samples; raw zero cadence stops the feet immediately. Motion/biome proof pages and capture scripts are in `docs/visual-polish/player/README.md`.
## Tests
`npx vitest run src/games/warmup` (terrain integral/bounds, biome loop, determinism, flat toggle, startDistance, scenarios).
## Drive-In, mesas, tumbleweeds, sounds
- Sim: `receive({ type: 'driveIn', on })`, opt `driveIn`, tunable `driveInS`; snapshot `driveIn` (0..1), `from/to/mix`, `blendM`. Renderer treats the Drive-In as one more weighted look (`render/driveIn.ts`: look, screen frame, projector beam, trailer in `rider.ts`). The shell calls `renderer.setScreen(rect)` (CSS px) every frame; the frame is drawn after the ridge, before the ground.
- `mesa.ts`: seeded buttes (stepped tiers, jagged cliffs, talus, strata, sun-side shading, 3 depths with haze). Pure shape tests.
- `tumbleweed.ts`: pure motion (`weedAt`: bounce parabola, spin = ground distance / radius), seeded spawn; lanes far/road/near are drawn behind / in front of the rider.
- `soundscape.ts`: pure scheduler of per-biome one-shots (recipes in `audio/sfx.ts`: FM chirps via `vib`, swells via `atk`), weighted crossfade, silent when stopped or ducked (`ambientDucked()`).
- Debug: `?biome=desert&at=0.9` starts the lobby ride in a biome (km in), `?hour=21` previews the time of day.

## Achievement curve
Sightseer remains the introduction. Moonwalker needs five rides that visit the
Moon; Globetrotter needs every biome on three rides each. Both require ten active
minutes per ride, and duplicate biome events within a ride count only once.
Globetrotter uses a new packed, saturating per-biome ride counter; the old bitmap
cannot establish repeated visits. Existing earned rewards remain unlocked.
